Environmental Studies (EVS) - Practical Activities

Activity 1 - Nature Photography (TPS)

Participants will explore nearby natural areas to capture images of landscapes, wildlife, and biodiversity. This activity aims to enhance observational skills and foster an appreciation for the beauty and complexity of ecosystems.

Activity 2 - Documentary on Environmental Pollution (TPS)

Students will watch and critically analyze a documentary focusing on the causes, effects, and potential solutions to environmental pollution. Discussions will follow to deepen understanding and encourage innovative thinking.

Activity 3 - Nature Walk (TPS)

A guided walk through local natural areas will allow participants to observe and learn about native flora and fauna. This activity emphasizes the importance of biodiversity and ecological balance in sustaining healthy ecosystems.

Activity 4 - Herbarium Making (TPS)

Students will collect, press, and preserve plant specimens to create a herbarium. This hands-on activity will help participants understand plant taxonomy, diversity, and the role of plants in ecological systems.

Activity 5 - Awareness Campaign (TPS)

Through creative initiatives such as flash mobs, street plays, and public awareness drives, students will engage with the community to highlight pressing environmental issues and inspire collective action for sustainability.

Activity 6 - Survey of Endangered Himalayan Plants (TPS)

Participants will conduct field surveys to identify and document endangered plant species in the Himalayan region. This activity aims to contribute to conservation efforts and raise awareness about the importance of preserving biodiversity. Maximum pages: 2 (without cover). Minimum 10 species. Identification of species, region, significance and reason for being declared as endangered.

Activity 7 - Environmental Energy Audit (TPS)

Students will assess energy usage within the campus or hostel facilities to identify areas for improvement. This activity promotes energy efficiency and sustainable practices in daily operations.

Activity 8 - Carbon Footprint Estimation (TPS)

Participants will calculate the carbon footprint of the campus or specific sections of it. This activity aims to provide insights into greenhouse gas emissions and encourage strategies for reduction.

Activity 9 - Best Out of Waste Workshop (TPS)

Students will learn creative ways to repurpose waste materials into useful or decorative items. This workshop emphasizes the importance of recycling and reducing waste to minimize environmental impact.
